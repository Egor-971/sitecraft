# Product requirements and release scope

Sitecraft grew from requests for purposeful, individual websites with professional UI/UX and frontend execution. A site must perform its actual business function: promote an offer, sell an item, teach, communicate, provide media or serve as the product itself.

The plugin distinguishes public promotion from functional applications. Five skills cover creation, product implementation, redesign, review and professional upgrades. The 24 task profiles cover commerce, communication, learning, media, workspaces and new niches; they are guidance, not complete ready-made systems.

Research uses current relevant competitors and honest measurements where accessible. Familiar names, logos and screenshots are references for concrete design principles, not instructions to copy brand identity or redistribute assets. Blocked regional pages are reported, without invented CSS values.

Baseline quality includes readable localized content, coherent type/spacing, preserved useful functionality, complete scoped journeys, browser inspection and correction of material defects. An optional professional stage needs a concrete proposal and consent unless already requested. Publication and external actions follow the user's actual authorization.

Version 1.3 improves entrypoint clarity, first-delivery checks, official-source attribution, package diagnostics and public archive hygiene. English skill entrypoints and plugin metadata support international use; existing research notes retain their original language. Sites follow the requested audience and locale.

Future improvements will be incremental and evidence-driven. Detailed development fixtures and raw conversation remain private. No universal first-try guarantee, AI-authorship detector, conversion claim or vendor certification is offered.
