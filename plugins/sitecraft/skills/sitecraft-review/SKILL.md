# Sitecraft: review a website

Audit the available evidence concretely. Assess purpose, journey and presentation using [design rules](../../references/design-rules.md), [first delivery](../../references/first-delivery.md) and relevant [QA](../../references/qa.md). Find an application's applicable [profile](../../references/product-patterns.md); use the [reference board](../../references/reference-board.md) for a specific comparison.

## Review

- Establish the task, known requirements and evidence: screenshot, browser or source. Do not impose minimalism or infer authorship from appearance.
- On screenshots, inspect hierarchy, meaning, density, spacing, type roles, colors, materials and overlap. Do not claim exact CSS pixels, actual loaded font, interactivity or motion from an image.
- In the browser, complete the accessible primary path, keyboard controls, narrow layout and important states. For products, inspect data, persistence, direct URLs, refresh, errors and recovery within the audit scope. When source is available, inspect actual handlers and integration boundaries. Record conditions and inputs.
- For each finding, give its location, observation, consequence and remedy. Distinguish confirmed findings from hypotheses. Prioritize functionality and comprehension, then legibility and coherent design. Do not object to useful cards, icons, branded capitals or colors merely because they exist.
- Assess identity through content, composition and behavior across the authorized scope. Comparative research uses current primary sources and explicit limits. Do not default to praise or promise conversion gains. Evaluate the actual audience's language and copy.

## Result

For an audit-only request, deliver material findings and coverage without editing the site. If fixes are requested, implement and retest affected paths. An audit/proposal is not implementation. An explicit professional UI/UX request authorizes the relevant [stage](../sitecraft-elevate/SKILL.md) without another question; an optional proposal follows the [protocol](../../references/design-upgrade.md).

If no defects are found, say so with the exact scope of inspection. One screenshot does not validate the whole product; absence of generic patterns proves neither authorship nor the quality of every future generation.
