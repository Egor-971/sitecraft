# Code of Conduct

Sitecraft welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where people represent Sitecraft.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and critique the work, not the person.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and consider different perspectives.
- Respect privacy, asset licenses and other people's contributions.
- Keep discussion relevant and help newcomers participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, spam and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

The project maintainer is [Egor-971](https://github.com/Egor-971). For routine project moderation, open an issue describing the behavior without repeating sensitive information. For harassment or other sensitive abuse, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls; do not publish private incident details in an issue. Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may ask for a correction, remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads or restrict participation based on context and impact. Reports and responses should disclose only the information needed to resolve the issue. This small project does not promise a fixed moderation response time.
